Research focus

Three things I spend my time on.

Network tomography

Estimating internal link characteristics — delay, loss, structure — from end-to-end observations, when direct measurement of the interior isn't available.

Optimization

The machinery that makes those estimates tractable: formulating the problem so a solution exists, and finding it at a scale that's useful.

Teaching

Six courses as a teaching assistant at UCLA, from introductory statistical reasoning through Monte Carlo methods and consulting practice.

About

Statistics, slowly and thoroughly.

I came to statistics through applied mathematics and, less predictably, German language and culture — two degrees I finished at Cal State Long Beach before starting my PhD at UCLA in 2022.

I like problems where the answer is hiding behind what you're allowed to observe, and I like taking the time to actually understand a method rather than just apply it.
