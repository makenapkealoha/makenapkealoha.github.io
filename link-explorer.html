Interactive · Network tomography

Link-delay explorer

Fourteen perfSONAR monitors in the Merit network each probe the other thirteen. Pick a monitor to see its reduced candidate design on the map, every super-link sorted by true delay, and where non-negative least squares (NNLS) puts that delay.

Loading the explorer…

How to read it

  • Top-4 super-link (largest true delay)
  • Super-link with delay > 0
  • Super-link with delay = 0
  • NNLS estimate (diamonds, right panel)

Interacting

Use the drop-down at the top left to switch monitor. Hover any link on the map to see its true delay, rank, NNLS estimate, the raw links inside it and their delay range across monitors, the cities at either end and the distance. The link glows and its bar on the right is highlighted; hovering a bar works the other way round. Hover a router for its hostname and location.

About the data. One day of traceroute measurements (8 September 2023), full routing regime. A link's delay is the median per-hop increase in round-trip time. Routers are placed at the city in their hostname; the few without one are placed at the centre of their neighbours. Best viewed on a laptop or larger screen.
