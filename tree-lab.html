Forking-tree lab

Build a forking tree from one root monitor, give every link a delay, and see how ordinary least squares (OLS), the LASSO, non-negative least squares (NNLS) and stability-selected NNLS reconstruct those delays from simulated end-to-end route measurements. Everything runs in your browser, and the results update as you edit.
