Maintaining this page

How to add material.

Everything on this page comes from one array. Open js/supplementary.js and edit LIBRARY. Give an entry a url and it becomes a live link with your chosen badge; leave url off and it renders greyed out as To write, so the page doubles as a checklist of what's still missing. Put PDFs and note files in the notes/ folder and point at them, for example notes/mcmc-diagnostics.pdf.
